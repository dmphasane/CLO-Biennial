QUICK START
───────────
1. Sign in with your role credentials.
2. Go to Members > Add Member  (or Import CSV using sample_members_template.csv).
3. Go to Bank Statements > Upload Statement  (use sample_bank_statement.csv to test).
4. Check Exceptions tab for any unmatched or flagged entries and resolve them.
5. Go to Reports > Generate Monthly / Quarterly / YTD reports.
6. Export reports as Excel, CSV, or PDF for governance review.

DATA STORAGE
────────────
All data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage.
Data persists between sessions on the same browser/device.
To back up data, export member register and reports regularly.

EXCEPTION STATUS REFERENCE
────────────────────────────
  MATCHED          – Payment matched and allocated successfully
  PARTIAL_REF      – Reference partially matched; requires manual confirmation
  UNMATCHED        – No matching member found
  DUPLICATE        – More than one payment received for same member/month
  AMOUNT_VARIANCE  – Amount differs from expected by more than R0.05
  PARTIAL_PAYMENT  – Amount is less than 90% of expected
  EXCESS_PAYMENT   – Amount exceeds expected by more than R0.05
  OUT_OF_PERIOD    – Transaction falls outside Jun 2026 – May 2027
