2. How to Register

Open the registration form here:

https://clo-biennial.onrender.com/register

Step 1 — Your details

  • Full Name — exactly as it should appear on your registration
  • Conference — ECLO, NCLO, WCLO, OCLO or MMMCLO
  • District — the list updates based on your conference
  • Station (Local Church) — the list updates based on your district
  • Email address and Contact number
Can't find your district or station in the list? Choose “My station is not listed” (or “Other district”) and type it in. It will be saved for the next person from your church.

Step 2 — Choose your accommodation option

Pick the option that suits you. Each card shows your monthly instalment, your total commitment, and the payment period. Options include:

  • New hotels: The Capital and The Catalyst (Single or Sharing B&B)
  • Existing hotels: Sandton Sun, Sandton Towers, Garden Court (Sharing / Not Sharing)
  • Registration Only: no accommodation

Step 3 — Submit

Review your summary and click Submit Registration. You'll see a success screen and receive a confirmation email with everything you need.

4. After You Register

Immediately after submitting:

  • You receive a confirmation email with your option, payment schedule, reference, and banking details. A copy is also sent to the NEDLO office.
  • Your registration is recorded centrally and tracked for the full savings period.
  • As you pay each month, your contributions are matched to your record and reflected in your statement.

You may receive periodic contribution statements by email showing your payments and any outstanding balance.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

I registered but didn't get an email.

Check your spam/junk folder. If it's not there, contact the Financial Secretary to confirm your email address on file.

I used the wrong reference on a payment.

Contact the Financial Secretary with the date and amount so your payment can be allocated correctly.

It says my reference already exists.

You may already be registered. Contact the Financial Secretary rather than registering again, to avoid duplicate records.

Can I change my accommodation option?

Yes — contact the Financial Secretary. Changes are handled by the office.

My church isn't in the dropdown.

Select “My station is not listed” and type it in. It will then be available for others.

6. For Office-Bearers (Overview)

Authorised office-bearers manage the fund through a secure admin portal. At a high level, the portal lets the team:

  • View all registered members and the overall fund dashboard
  • Upload FNB bank statements and automatically match payments to members
  • Review and resolve any payments that need attention
  • Generate monthly, quarterly and year-to-date reconciliation reports
  • Allocate hotel rooms and email members their contribution statements
Access is restricted to authorised personnel and protected under POPIA. Login credentials are issued privately and must never be shared. Detailed operating instructions are provided to office-bearers separately.
